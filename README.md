# Velvet & Vow: concept website

A single-page site for **Velvet & Vow**, a fictional wedding design, décor and rentals house in Trinidad. It sells all-inclusive wedding packages, rents décor, and styles weddings, Hindu weddings, beach ceremonies, birthdays and showers.

Concept build by **Portside Digital**: websites that work. Velvet & Vow is not a real business.

**Look:** Bodoni Moda, Pinyon Script and Jost, in a night-reception palette of plum black, wine velvet, blush, rose and champagne gold.

**Scroll craft** (GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis, turned off for people who prefer reduced motion):
- Hero: wine velvet curtains with a gold title. Scrolling gathers the drapes to the sides with gold tiebacks, zooms out from a chandelier ballroom, and fires cold-spark fountains from both corners on a canvas
- Velocity-reactive marquee, and a statement that lights up word by word
- **The transformation**: a pinned illustrated reception hall builds itself in seven scroll steps (draping, head table and thrones with a monogram dance floor, chiavari-chair guest tables, florals, chandeliers and uplighting, then fog and cold sparks). Five theme chips (Black & gold, Blush dream, Red romance, Royal purple, Garden white) restyle the whole room live and carry the choice into the enquiry form
- All-inclusive packages for 50, 75, 100 or 150 guests. A sliding selector tweens the price and the per-guest cost and fills a 150-seat dot grid
- Décor closet: a pinned horizontal rental gallery on desktop (native swipe on mobile). "Add" builds a quote tray that feeds the enquiry message
- Looks: a pinned zoom-out where one full-screen photo shrinks into a 3×3 grid
- Event cards with clip-path reveals, a gold thread that draws through the four-step process, and an enquiry form whose live WhatsApp bubble writes the message as you type, over falling rose petals

**Mobile:** sticky bottom dock (call / check your date / WhatsApp), safe-area padding, 16px inputs, hover styles gated to mouse.

Call, WhatsApp and social actions show a demo message instead of contacting anyone.
